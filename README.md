# 🎬 WellPlayed

**Watch anything together. Movies, lectures, live sports, or a YouTube playlist in the background.**

WellPlayed is a complete platform for watching together. One shared core handles sync, chat, languages and wellbeing, and dedicated modes tailor the experience to how your group is watching: a family movie night across five homes, a study group working through a lecture, mates following a match, or friends hanging out with videos playing in the background.

> *Whatever you're watching, watch it together.*

---

## 🤔 The problem

Existing watch-party apps (Teleparty, Kosmi, Flickcall and others) solved basic sync and chat for movies. They still leave groups stuck on:

- **Cost.** Every viewer is assumed to need their own subscription.
- **Choosing.** Groups waste time working out what everyone can actually access, especially across regions.
- **Interruptions.** One person's buffering or break pauses everyone.
- **Language barriers.** Multilingual groups share one subtitle track and one chat language.
- **One-size-fits-all.** A lecture, a live match and a movie night need very different tools, but every app treats them the same.
- **Unhealthy sessions.** Hours of slouched, unblinking watching, with nothing nudging anyone to move.

WellPlayed tackles all six.

## 🧱 The Core (in every mode)

### ⏯️ Smarter Sync
- **Pause requests.** Members tap "need a break" instead of pausing for everyone. The host approves, or the room auto-pauses with a countdown ("Resuming in 2:00 — Uzi's grabbing snacks").
- **Buffer forecasting.** WellPlayed monitors each member's network health and preloads ahead for the weakest connection, so any pause happens before a stall rather than during one.
- **Late-joiner catch-up.** Anyone joining partway through gets an AI-generated, spoiler-safe recap of everything up to that exact moment.

### 📱 Companion Phones
Watch on the big screen and chat, talk, react and vote from your phone, so the main screen stays clean.

### 🌏 Multilingual by Default
- **Chat auto-translation.** Everyone writes in their own language and reads in theirs.
- **Per-user subtitles.** Each viewer picks their own subtitle language and size.

### 🧍 "Are You Still Human?" — Healthy Watching
*Netflix asks "Are you still watching?" WellPlayed asks "Are you still human?"*

- **Playful transformations.** Slouch and you turn into a shrimp. Stop blinking and you become a fish. Sit through two episodes straight and you're a potato. Everyone in the party can see it.
- **Next-episode lock.** The next episode only starts once everyone stands and completes a 30-second stretch. If one person skips it, the whole group waits, so positive peer pressure does the work.
- **Private and inclusive.** Opt-in, with all camera processing on the user's device; no video ever leaves it. Seated-stretch alternatives and a host setting make it work for everyone, whatever their mobility.

### 🍿 Reely, your AI watch-party host
- **A private host for everyone.** Each person gets their own chat with Reely: trivia, spoiler-free predictions, mini-games ("two truths and a lie", "guess the next line"), conversation starters and a recap of the room chat.
- **Knows the room, not your screen.** Reely sees the title, the room chat and, if a transcript was uploaded, what was said up to *your* position, never beyond it. Spoiler-safe mode is on by default.
- **Polls in one tap.** Reely drafts polls you can share with the whole room. Pick a personality: Quiet, Normal or Chaotic.
- **Works offline.** Without an API key, a built-in Reely still runs every quick prompt.

### 🛡️ Party controls
- **End or leave cleanly.** The host can end the party for everyone; anyone can leave. If the host leaves, the longest-present member takes over.
- **Safe by default.** Rooms have a participant limit, close when nobody has been watching for a while, and rate-limit floods of messages, reactions and video changes.
- **Honest about links.** Links are checked before they load. For services that can't be embedded (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Tubi, Plex and others), WellPlayed explains why and offers to open it there with a group countdown on every screen, or the Household extension.
- **Synced speed.** The host sets 0.5× to 2× and every player follows.

### 🎉 Engagement & Replay Value
- **Live polls.** Prediction polls, quizzes and votes, adapted to each mode.
- **Reaction heatmap.** Afterwards, a timeline shows where the group laughed, cheered, screamed or went quiet.
- **End-of-party awards.** Awards earned from what really happened: The Reaction Machine, Chatterbox Supreme, The Oracle, The Emotional Support Potato 🥔 and more. Add peer-voted awards ("The Plot-Twist Goblin") and a quick ⭐ review of what you watched, then reveal them together.
- **Hall of Fame.** Revealed awards are kept on your device, so past parties' winners live on.
- **Highlight reel** *(stretch goal).* Opt-in auto-clips of the group's best reaction moments.

## 🎛️ Modes

### 🏠 Movie Night & Household Mode
*A 20-person family movie night shouldn't need 20 subscriptions.*

- **Household Mode.** Sync homes, not individuals. Each household plays the film on its own TV from its own account, and WellPlayed keeps every TV in sync to the second. Five living rooms feel like one couch.
- **Smart Coverage Planner.** Members are grouped by household with the services each home has. WellPlayed picks the service that covers the most homes at the lowest extra cost, and suggests the cheapest fix for any gaps: join a nearby relative's home, add an extra member, or switch to a free service such as SBS On Demand or ABC iview.
- **"What can we all watch?"** Recommends titles available to everyone, accounting for regional catalogue differences.
- **Swipe-to-vote.** Everyone suggests up to five titles, then swipes on each one. Votes stay hidden until the host reveals them, ties go to a tie-breaker round, and the winner loads straight into the player. You can also swipe through the sample catalogue.
- **Prediction polls.** "Who's the killer?" Votes are locked in and revealed at the end.

**Impact:** 20 people across 5 homes need only 5 subscriptions, cutting the group's cost by about 75%, fully within streaming platforms' rules.

### 🎓 Study Mode
*Lectures are easier to finish together.*

- **Synced lecture viewing** for study groups, from any recording.
- **Focus check.** Anyone who picks up their phone or starts dozing off turns into an animal, visible to the group.
- **Quiz breaks.** The video pauses at set intervals for an AI-generated or preplanned quiz. The fastest correct answer earns points on a group leaderboard.
- **AI lecture notes.** A summary of key points, linked to timestamps, at the end of each session.

### ⚽ Sports Mode
*Never have a goal spoiled by a friend's cheer.*

- **Spoiler-safe live sync.** Live streams arrive with different delays for different viewers. WellPlayed aligns everyone to the same moment, so nobody's reaction gives the play away.
- **Live predictions.** "Who scores next?" "Will they make the final?" Points tallied across the match.
- **Fan reactions.** Synced cheers, groans and celebrations that hit everyone's screen at the same moment.

### 🎧 Hangout Mode
*Videos in the background, friends in the foreground.*

- **Shared queue.** Everyone adds YouTube videos or music to one playlist and votes to skip.
- **Group vote.** Vote on what to queue next; the winner goes into the queue.
- **Audio-first.** Low-bandwidth, background-friendly playback so the conversation stays front and centre.
- **Drop-in, drop-out.** Join or leave any time without interrupting anyone.

### 🧭 Around the app
- **Discover.** Community recommendations with a fun poll on each, a searchable sample catalogue, and an honest list of which platforms sync today.
- **Reely AI** and **Hall of Fame** pages, plus **Settings**: light or dark theme, reduce motion, camera and mic defaults with a local camera test, chat sounds and recent rooms. Settings are saved on your device only.

## ⚖️ Playing by the rules

WellPlayed never restreams or screen-shares protected content. Every viewer or household plays from its own legitimate source, and WellPlayed only syncs playback, so it respects DRM and platforms' terms of service. The demo uses YouTube, royalty-free films and openly licensed lectures.

## 🚀 Getting started

**Prerequisites:** Python 3.12+ with [uv](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/), Node 22+ with pnpm. Docker is optional.

```bash
make setup        # installs backend (uv) and frontend (pnpm) dependencies
make backend      # API + WebSocket server on http://localhost:8000
make frontend      # web app on http://localhost:5173  (proxies /api and /ws to the backend)
```

Open http://localhost:5173, create a room, and open the invite link in a second browser window (or a private window) to join as another person. `frontend/public/demo.webm` is a 90 s clip you can load as a video URL: `http://localhost:5173/demo.webm`.

**Everything works without API keys.** Optional extras:

| Want | Do |
|---|---|
| AI recap / notes / quizzes / translation / Reely from an LLM | set `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (copy `.env.example` to `.env`). Without it, built-in extractive summaries, cloze quizzes and an offline Reely are used |
| Free offline translation | `cd backend && uv sync --extra translate`, then install Argos language packs |
| Try translation without any model | start the backend with `WP_TRANSLATOR=mock` |
| Voice and camera chat | `docker compose --profile voice up livekit`, keep the LiveKit values in `.env` |
| Transcribe a video file | `uv sync --extra asr` (faster-whisper) |
| Real catalogue availability | planned: TMDB. The bundled catalogue is sample data (see below) |
| Change room limits | `WP_MAX_PARTICIPANTS` (default 20), `WP_IDLE_ROOM_MINUTES` (30), `WP_ROOM_MAX_AGE_HOURS` (12) |
| Keep Discover recommendations somewhere else | `WP_DATA_DIR` (default `backend/var`, which is gitignored) |

### 🎬 Demo: watch a YouTube video with friends
The simplest use case, end to end. No API keys or accounts needed.

1. **Start it:** `make setup` once, then `make demo`. This builds the web app and serves everything on port 8000.
2. **Let friends in:**
   - **Same Wi-Fi:** they open `http://<your-computer's-IP>:8000`.
   - **Anywhere:** run `make share` in a second terminal (needs [cloudflared](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/connections/connect-networks/downloads/)) and send the `https://….trycloudflare.com` link it prints.
3. **Create a room:** on the home page, enter your name, pick a mode and click **Create room**.
4. **Invite:** click **Invite** and share the link, the QR code or the 6-letter code. Friends enter a name and click **Join**.
5. **Pick a video:** paste any YouTube link (watch, youtu.be, Shorts or a share link) and click **Load**, or click **Try a YouTube video**. Everyone's player loads it, and the title and thumbnail appear automatically.
6. **Press play:** everyone starts together. Pause, seek or skip ±10 s and everyone follows within a fraction of a second. The **In sync** pill shows your drift.
7. **Show it off:** chat (each person reads it in their own language), tap reactions so they float across everyone's screen, and tap **I need a break** to ask for a pause.
8. **Wrap up:** the host opens **Awards** and clicks **Reveal awards**, then **End** closes the party for everyone. Anyone can **Leave** at any time.

What happens when things go wrong:

| Situation | What people see |
|---|---|
| The browser blocks autoplay | A **Tap to join playback** button. One tap and that viewer is in sync |
| The video's owner disabled embedding, or it's private or removed | "This video can't play" with the reason. The host uses **Change video** |
| YouTube is blocked (network, ad blocker) | A clear message after ~12 s instead of a black box |
| A guest presses pause or seeks inside the YouTube player | They're kept in sync and told only the host controls playback (the host can allow everyone in **Room settings**) |
| The video ends | The room pauses on "Finished". Play starts it again from the top |
| Someone pastes a Netflix (or similar) link | Why it can't play in WellPlayed, plus **Open on Netflix (not synced)** and **Start a group countdown** |
| The room is full | The join page says so and shows the limit |
| The host leaves | The longest-present member becomes host and gets the controls straight away |
| The host ends the party | Everyone sees who ended it; the invite link stops working |
| Your session is no longer valid | "You're no longer in this party" with **Rejoin**, prefilled with your name |
| The server restarted | "This room has ended" with a button to start a new one |

`frontend/e2e/youtube.mjs` checks this whole flow in two real browsers. It uses a stand-in for YouTube's player API, so it runs offline.

### Try each feature (two browser windows)
The room has the video on the left and a tool rail on the right (Chat, People, the mode's own panel, Polls, Reely, Awards, Voice, then AI, Wellbeing, Subtitles, Highlights and Network). The top bar has a theme toggle and **Leave** for everyone; hosts also get **Invite**, **Pair phone**, a **Room settings** gear and **End**.

| Feature | How |
|---|---|
| Synced playback, pause requests | Host pastes a link or clicks **Try the demo clip**; guest taps **I need a break** |
| Chat translation / subtitles | Join with a different language; upload an `.srt` in **Subtitles** |
| Household coverage planner | Movie mode → **Movie night**: add households + services, then **Plan** |
| Group vote | Movie mode → **Movie night** → **Swipe** → **Our picks** (or Hangout → **Group vote**): add titles, host starts and reveals |
| Polls, predictions, heatmap | **Polls** (host creates), tap emoji reactions, **Highlights** |
| Late-joiner recap, notes, quiz breaks | Upload a transcript in **AI**; recap uses only what was said before the viewer's position |
| Healthy watching | **Wellbeing** → opt in → *Start camera*, or use the simulator buttons; turn on the stretch lock in **Room settings** and use **Next episode** |
| Sports spoiler safety | Sports mode → **Live sync**: turn on spoiler-safe, set different delays per window, send chat/reactions |
| Hangout queue | Hangout mode → **Queue** |
| Reely AI host | **Reely** → try the quick prompts, then **Share poll to room** |
| Awards and Hall of Fame | **Awards**: host picks categories and opens voting, everyone votes and reviews, host reveals; then visit **Hall of Fame** |
| End, leave, speed, link checks | **End** / **Leave** in the top bar; speed menu in the player; paste a Netflix link to see the fallback |
| Discover, Settings, light theme | Top nav on the home page |
| Companion phone | **Pair phone** and scan the QR (or open the link) |
| Household Mode on real services | load `extension/` (see `extension/README.md`) |

Keyboard: **Space** plays and pauses for anyone allowed to control playback; **← ↑ →** swipe in the movie-night deck; **← →** vote in a group vote.

### Status and honest limits
Built and tested locally: everything in the feature list above. Known limits of this first version:

- **State is in memory.** Rooms, Reely chats, rate limits and the list of ended parties live in the server process (restart = rooms gone). Discover recommendations are the exception: they're saved to `WP_DATA_DIR/recs.json`. The hub and room manager are written so Redis (fan-out) and Postgres (history) can be added; `docker-compose.yml` already has both services, but they are not wired in yet.
- **Highlight reel:** the *clip candidates* (time ranges around the biggest reactions) are computed; actually cutting video clips is not built.
- **Catalogue and prices are sample data** (`backend/app/data/`), marked as such in the UI. Real availability needs a provider such as TMDB.
- **Voice/video** is implemented against LiveKit but was only unit-tested (token issuing); it was not run against a live LiveKit server here.
- **The extension** was tested on the repo's own demo video page, not on real streaming services, which change often.
- **Wellbeing models** (MediaPipe) download from Google storage on first use; the pose logic is unit-tested with synthetic landmarks and the camera pipeline was run against a fake camera, not a real person.
- **Reely** was tested in offline mode and with a stand-in model, not yet with a live API key.
- **Awards and Hall of Fame** are saved per device (no accounts), so each person keeps their own copy.
- The app has no user accounts (guests join by link).
